## 1. What Gamma is: its exact mechanics and its status (live on mainnet, testnet, or proposal)

### Takeaway
Gamma is live on Finney mainnet. It is not a tradable token. It is a Subtensor runtime feature: a **per-subnet, US-dollar-valued, non-transferable, non-redeemable prepaid service credit**. A payer's coldkey mints it by burning that subnet's alpha. The alpha is either bought with TAO inside the same call or taken from stake, and it is valued through a TAO/USD oracle that root appoints. Gamma shipped in runtime 488 on 2026-11-05, and root switched it on network-wide on 2026-11-09. **Since runtime 493 (live 2026-11-20 17:46 UTC), the deposit's own API-key hotkey spends gamma fee-free at a published service.** The service then credits its own off-chain USD balance from an on-chain running total, and spending pays the provider nothing on chain. Each subnet must opt in. As of 2026-11-23, 13 subnets had opted in; **Chutes (SN64) and Targon (SN4) had not**.

#### What gamma is, per the code and docs (primary)
- "Gamma is a credit a subnet can accept as payment for its service, valued in US dollars... One gamma is minted for each dollar the burned alpha is worth." Amounts use "10^9 units per gamma" — [docs/guides/gamma.mdx (main, read 2026-11-23)](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/docs/guides/gamma.mdx).
- "Gamma cannot be transferred to another coldkey or converted back into alpha or TAO". It "confers no claim on any party and cannot be redeemed. A subnet decides whether, and at what prices, it accepts its gamma" — [gamma guide](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/docs/guides/gamma.mdx).
- **Expiry:** no time-based expiry appears in the code or docs. The only loss event is subnet dissolution: "Dissolving or pruning a subnet destroys all of its gamma and clears what was spent at its services, and holders receive nothing" — [gamma guide](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/docs/guides/gamma.mdx).
- Roles: the **coldkey** mints into its deposits, moves gamma between them, sets each deposit's daily spend cap, and closes empty deposits. The **hotkey** spends its deposit at the subnet's services without a fee and signs requests to them. A **validator or subnet-owner hotkey** publishes a service. The **subnet owner** sets `gamma_enabled` and can pay shares of emission into other subnets' gamma. **Root** enables gamma network-wide, whitelists gamma-emission targets and sets the oracle. The **oracle** feeds TAO/USD — [gamma guide](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/docs/guides/gamma.mdx).
- **Transferability, precisely:** gamma can move only between the same coldkey's own deposits. "To give someone gamma, open a deposit with their hotkey. The deposit stays yours: move the gamma back at any time" — [gamma guide](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/docs/guides/gamma.mdx).

#### Minting and valuation
- `mint_gamma` "buys alpha with the TAO and burns it, with the same pool and flow accounting as `stake-burn`, and creates no stake position". `mint_gamma_with_stake` burns alpha already staked. CLI: `btcli gamma mint --netuid N --hotkey API_KEY --amount-tao 10`; SDK: `bt.MintGamma(netuid=..., hotkey_ss58=..., amount_tao=...)` — [gamma guide](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/docs/guides/gamma.mdx).
- Valuation: `TAO value = alpha × min(gamma price, spot price)`; `gamma = TAO value × TAO/USD price`. Each subnet's gamma price "falls to the spot price at once and rises toward it by ln 2 / 600 of the gap, so the gap halves every 600 blocks, about 2 hours". It "at most doubles every 600 blocks". The spot price is read before the mint trades: "Alpha is valued at these prices, not at what selling it would return, so a burn that is large for the pool is worth more than its sale" — [gamma guide](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/docs/guides/gamma.mdx).
- Mint limits: minimum 0.1 gamma (`AmountTooLow`). It fails with `GammaDisabled` if gamma is off network-wide or on the subnet; with `SlippageTooHigh` below the caller's minimum; with `InsufficientLiquidity` above the add-stake swap input limit; and with `TaoUsdPriceUnavailable` if the oracle feed is more than 300 blocks old. "The SDK quotes the mint first and fails it if the minted gamma would fall more than `rate_tolerance` (5% by default) below the quote" — [gamma guide](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/docs/guides/gamma.mdx).
- Enabling: `gamma_enabled` (`SubnetGammaEnabled`) defaults to false and is set by the subnet owner or root. The owner is rate-limited and refused during the admin freeze window. A mint needs both `GammaEnabled` (root) and `SubnetGammaEnabled`. "Turning one off stops new mints"; existing deposits can still be spent, moved and closed — [gamma_enabled docs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/docs/hyperparameters/gamma-enabled.mdx); [gamma guide](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/docs/guides/gamma.mdx).

#### Deposits, API keys and caps
- Gamma is held in deposits keyed `(netuid, hotkey)`, one per hotkey per subnet. A coldkey holds at most 64 per subnet. "Any account can be a deposit's hotkey, and it needs no registration or funds... create a fresh hotkey for each deposit. Requests signed with it are paid from the deposit, so never use a staking hotkey or a coldkey" — [gamma guide](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/docs/guides/gamma.mdx).
- PR #3314 (merged 2026-11-10) "restores the original Gamma design": a deposit's API-key hotkey could be unassociated or owned by another coldkey, and the funding coldkey keeps control of the deposit — [PR #3314](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/pull/3314). The current guide narrows this. The hotkey "can be unassociated", but "If it is already associated with a coldkey, only that coldkey can open a deposit at it... This prevents someone else from reserving an owned hotkey's deposit slot". "Moving the balance to another API key revokes access to those credits through the old key" — [gamma guide](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/docs/guides/gamma.mdx).
- Daily spend caps refill linearly over 7,200 blocks (about a day). A cap of 0 pauses the deposit, and any other cap is at least 0.1 gamma. "While a deposit has a cap, its hotkey spends at most the highest cap plus its refill over any period." `move_gamma` takes the whole balance, or at least 0.1 gamma while leaving at least as much — [gamma guide](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/docs/guides/gamma.mdx).

#### Spending and how a provider accounts for it (runtime 493, live 2026-11-20)
- PR #3343 merged 2026-11-19 and shipped in Release v493, which merged 2026-11-20. It says: "A gamma deposit is now spent by its own hotkey. The hotkey signs `spend_gamma(service, netuid, amount)` at a service listed on the subnet, pays no fee, and the amount is burned and added to `GammaSpent(netuid, service, hotkey)`. A service credits a hotkey with what that total has grown by, so it sends no transaction and holds no spending authority over its customers' deposits" — [PR #3343](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/pull/3343); [PR #3345](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/pull/3345).
- These were breaking changes. Call indices 158, 159, 162, 164 and 165 were retired. `mint_gamma`, `mint_gamma_with_stake` and `set_service` moved to 166, 167 and 169 without a spender argument, and `spend_gamma` is 168. `set_gamma_deposit_spender` was removed, and "`ProxyType::GammaSpend` is deprecated and denies all calls". `GammaRuntimeApi` moved to v2. A spend is "at least 0.1 gamma unless it empties the deposit". "A hotkey has one spend pending per subnet". The migration "pauses the subnet owner's uncapped deposit at the subnet owner hotkey and any cap below 0.1 gamma" — [PR #3343](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/pull/3343); [v493 release notes JSON](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/.github/release-notes/runtime/493.json).
- Provider accounting rule: "A service keeps each hotkey's credit itself and sends no transaction. At a finalized block it reads `gamma-spent` for the hotkey at each of its hotkeys in `publishers`, and credits what each total has grown by since its last read". Because dissolution clears the totals, "a service stores the subnet's `RegisteredSubnetCounter` with each total" — [services guide](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/docs/guides/services.mdx).
- "Spent gamma pays the service nothing on chain: the burned alpha's value accrues to the subnet's alpha holders" — [gamma guide](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/docs/guides/gamma.mdx). Release 487 says the same: "It does not transfer TAO or alpha to the provider; the alpha burn occurs when the credits are minted" — [PR #3294](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/pull/3294).
- Before v493 (runtimes 488–492), a provider's "spender" account, or a `GammaSpend` proxy, charged deposits with batched `spend_gamma(netuid, entries)` (up to 512 entries, each carrying a 32-byte reference). The internal 2026-11-11 design ([BTCPAY.md §18.4](/Users/brien/urnetwork/mmm/BTCPAY.md)) was built on that model, which v493 superseded. **URnetwork's server already follows v493:** server commit `5ca596d12` is titled "Pay with Gamma on runtime 493: each charge is spent by the deposit's own hotkey". The header of gamma_chain.go says "Runtime 493 retired the spender, the GammaSpend proxy and the spend reference" — [internal] [gamma_chain.go](/Users/brien/urnetwork/server/controller/gamma_chain.go).

#### Service discovery and request signing
- A validator-permit hotkey, or the subnet owner for its owner hotkey, publishes `set_service(netuid, url)`. The url (at most 256 bytes) points to a JSON document with `name`, `description`, `publishers`, `services` (openapi/http/mcp), and `payments` of type `gamma` (with `balance`, `signature: "btauth/1"`, `max_spend`) and/or `x402`. "Customers spend only at listed hotkeys, since anyone can publish the url" — [services guide](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/docs/guides/services.mdx); [PR #3322](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/pull/3322).
- With `btauth/1`, "The hotkey that spent signs each request, with the service's hotkey as the receiver, and the service charges what that hotkey spent at it" — [services guide](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/docs/guides/services.mdx). The signed-requests scheme uses `X-Bittensor-*` headers over an eight-line canonical string (method, target, body hash, nanosecond nonce, sender, receiver) with a 10-second window — [internal] report citing [signed-requests guide](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/docs/guides/signed-requests.mdx).

#### Gamma emission (subnets paying subnets from emissions)
- PR #3320 (merged 2026-11-10) lets a subnet owner give shares of its alpha emission to other subnets' gamma. Each share is at most 10%, there are at most 16 targets, and targets must be whitelisted by root. "At each epoch the subnet sells the alpha its shares accrued, buys the target's alpha with the TAO, burns it and credits the gamma to the subnet owner's deposit at the owner hotkey on the target", within ±2% price bands — [PR #3320](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/pull/3320).
- Live state [chain 2026-11-23]: the gamma-emission whitelist is {SN51 Lium, SN53 engy}. Four subnets have configured shares: SN3 "Teutonic" gives 5% to SN51; SN97 "Ingot" gives 5% to SN51; **SN120 "Affine" gives 10% to SN51**; SN76 "Ormas" gives 5% to SN53. The shares were decoded from `u64::MAX` fractions. On 2026-11-10 the whitelist had been empty — [internal] report.

#### The TAO/USD oracle
- "An oracle set by root feeds the TAO/USD price... The accepted price falls to a lower feed at once and rises toward a higher one by at most 0.0066% (66 ppm) per block, about 2% per hour". A mint "needs a feed at most 300 blocks (one hour) old". "The oracle is trusted: its accepted price can rise at most about 61% per day, until root replaces or removes it" — [gamma guide](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/docs/guides/gamma.mdx).

### Inferences
- For URnetwork's design, gamma is a **procurement instrument, not a payment token**. URnetwork would mint SN-X gamma from its own TAO into deposits keyed by API-key hotkeys it controls, then spend at SN-X's service. The provider credits a USD balance. No value flows to the provider on chain; the provider is paid by its own alpha appreciating and by emissions. Gamma is not something end users would hold.
- Since v493, every API-key hotkey can burn its whole deposit without further authorization, up to the daily cap. URnetwork's servers therefore hold "spend keys" whose blast radius is the deposit balance. **Daily caps and small per-key balances are the main risk control.** The coldkey (treasury) can pause any deposit with a zero cap or move the balance away.
- Gamma minted with TAO is **asymmetric against the payer** in fast markets. The accepted TAO/USD price falls immediately but rises at most about 2%/hour, and the subnet gamma price falls immediately but recovers only gradually. Minting just after a TAO or alpha rally yields fewer gamma per TAO; minting after a drop yields the drop at once. A treasury should mint in calm periods and in small, frequent tranches.
- The internal 2026-11-11 recommendation was to hold off until the TAO/USD oracle reliably fed within 300 blocks. The sampled history now points to a single ≈17-hour outage on 2026-11-10/11 and fresh feeds since. Minting is still a single point of failure on one root-appointed feed, so a payment flow should check `blocks_since_last_feed` before quoting and fall back to card/fiat rails when the feed is stale.
- Because gamma has no expiry but is destroyed if the subnet is dissolved or pruned, the stranded-balance risk is subnet survival plus whether the provider keeps honoring its service. "Confers no claim on any party" means there is no contractual recourse via the chain. Any recourse comes from the provider's own terms.

### Gaps
- No primary OpenTensor Foundation blog post or BIT document specifically about Gamma was found. The press "launch" items (2026-11-11) cite X posts and newsflashes. The repo docs and PRs are the primary specification. The Exploit Summit talk video and transcript were not found, so its content is known only from tao.media, Crypto Briefing and KuCoin summaries.

## 3. How a US business acquires TAO, alpha or Gamma with fiat and manages that treasury

### Takeaway
A US company can buy TAO for USD on **Coinbase** (spot since Feb 2025; Coinbase Custody Trust lists TAO; no staking) or **Kraken** (TAO, plus SN64 Chutes alpha since 2026-07-07). It can custody TAO with **BitGo** (TAO custody in MPC cold wallets) or self-custody a coldkey. **Gamma itself cannot be bought.** It can only be minted on chain, in one call that spends TAO to buy and burn the subnet's alpha, so no alpha leg or alpha custody is needed. Pool price impact on mint is negligible at Chutes, Targon and Lium depth (about 0.03% for $10k) but material on small subnets (Cathedral about 2.2% for $10k). The main treasury risks are TAO volatility while it is held, the oracle's lagging upward price, non-refundability, and per-subnet lock-in. In-scope crypto assets (TAO, alpha) are measured at fair value under ASU 2023-08, and every mint is a taxable disposition of TAO.

#### Accounting and tax (US)
- **ASU 2023-08 (ASC 350-60)** covers assets that:
  - "Meet the definition of intangible asset";
  - "Do not provide the asset holder with enforceable rights to or claims on underlying goods, services or other assets";
  - "Are created or reside on a distributed ledger";
  - "Are secured through cryptography";
  - "Are fungible";
  - "Are not created or issued by the reporting entity or its related parties".
  
  They are measured "at fair value with changes in fair value recognized in net income". The standard is effective "for fiscal years beginning after December 15, 2024", and early adoption is permitted — [RSM](https://rsmus.com/insights/financial-reporting/fasb-issues-asu-on-accounting-and-disclosure-of-certain-crypto-assets.html); [Deloitte DART](https://dart.deloitte.com/USDART/home/publications/deloitte/heads-up/2024/faq-fasb-crypto-assets-standard-asu-2023-08).
- **IRS:** "If you pay for a service using virtual currency that you hold as a capital asset, then you" have a capital gain or loss (Q14). "Your gain or loss is the difference between the fair market value of the services you received" and your adjusted basis (Q15). Fair market value is set by exchange records, by a blockchain explorer for peer-to-peer transactions, or by the value of the services received when there is no published value (Q26–Q28). The page was last reviewed 2026-06-30 and notes that its FAQs "generally apply to transactions completed before Jan. 1, 2025" — [IRS virtual currency FAQ](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/frequently-asked-questions-on-virtual-currency-transactions).

### Inferences
- **A treasury flow consistent with these facts:**
  1. Card, IAP and auto-renew receipts in USD collect in a bank account.
  2. Buy TAO weekly or daily on Coinbase Prime or Kraken.
  3. Withdraw to a URnetwork treasury coldkey (multisig/Ledger, or BitGo MPC if qualified custody is needed). Exchange custodians cannot sign `mint_gamma`.
  4. For gamma-enabled providers, `mint_gamma` just-in-time into one capped API-key hotkey per provider and environment, then spend at the provider's listed service hotkey.
  5. For Chutes, pay by card or Stripe (simplest), or by a TAO transfer to the account's `payment_address`.
  6. For Targon, negotiate with sales.
- Holding gamma or provider balances converts TAO risk into USD-denominated but **non-refundable, single-subnet** credit risk. Keep prefunding to days of expected usage, not months.
- Lot-level basis tracking is needed because every mint (and every TAO payment to Chutes) is a taxable disposition. Burns are irreversible, so the "fair value of services received" equals the gamma minted (USD) under Q28-style reasoning.
- Gamma itself probably fails ASU 2023-08 scope in practice. It is non-transferable, has no market, and cannot be sold, so fair value through net income is ill-suited. A natural treatment is **prepaid expense (or immediate expense) at mint**, then consumption. This needs accountant confirmation.
- At Chutes, Targon and Lium depth, mint slippage is immaterial at URnetwork scale. Price-lag rules and fees dominate. At thin subnets (Cathedral, Terza, Attune), spread mints over time and cap the size of any single mint.

### Gaps

- Kraken alpha withdrawal mechanics (to a coldkey as stake) were not documented in the listing post. Live US availability of Kraken's other subnet tokens was not checked.
- No US-regulated TAO futures for hedging were identified.
- No authoritative source treats gamma or burned alpha under ASC 350-60 or the tax rules. IRS 1099-DA broker-reporting details for TAO were not checked.
- How other resellers (e.g. OpenRouter routing to Chutes) pay Bittensor providers was not researched.

## 4. Regulatory and platform rules for selling a fiat-funded prepaid compute balance redeemable for third-party services paid in crypto

### Takeaway
Under FinCEN rules, a URnetwork compute balance usable only for URnetwork's own services, with no cash-out, no user-to-user transfer, and no direct redemption at third parties, fits **"closed loop prepaid access"**. An arrangement capped at **$2,000 per device per day** is not a "prepaid program". Separately, accepting funds "integral to the sale of goods or the provision of services" is excluded from money transmission. URnetwork paying Bittensor providers in TAO or gamma for its own procurement is a purchase, not transmission on customers' behalf. The design should avoid any feature that lets users direct value to third parties or withdraw it. Apple requires IAP for digital credits outside the US storefront, and says IAP credits "may not expire". US storefront apps may link out to web purchase, with no Apple commission currently approved after the Dec 2025 Ninth Circuit ruling. Google no longer requires Play Billing for US users, but alternative billing and link-outs carry fees from 2026-10-01. OFAC liability is strict, which favors KYC and geo-blocking on the fiat side.

### Cited Findings

#### FinCEN (31 CFR 1010.100)
- "(ww) Prepaid access. Access to funds or the value of funds that have been paid in advance and can be retrieved or transferred at some point in the future through an electronic device or vehicle such as a card, code, electronic serial number, mobile identification number, or personal identification number." — [31 CFR 1010.100 (Cornell LII)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/31/1010.100).
- "(kkk) Closed loop prepaid access. Prepaid access to funds or the value of funds that can be used only for goods or services in transactions involving a defined merchant or location (or set of locations)..." — [31 CFR 1010.100](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/31/1010.100).
- (ff)(4)(iii): an arrangement is not a prepaid program if "(A) It provides closed loop prepaid access to funds not to exceed $2,000 maximum value that can be associated with a prepaid access device or vehicle on any day". The $1,000 open-loop exclusion in (D) also requires no international transmission, no transfers among users, and no loads from non-depository sources — [31 CFR 1010.100](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/31/1010.100).
- (ff)(7) Seller of prepaid access: this covers anyone selling prepaid access usable before customer verification, or selling prepaid access "(including closed loop prepaid access) to funds that exceed $10,000 to any person during any one day" without "policies and procedures reasonably adapted to prevent such a sale" — [31 CFR 1010.100](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/31/1010.100).
- (ff)(5)(ii): "money transmitter" excludes a person that only "(B) Acts as a payment processor to facilitate the purchase of... a good or service through a clearance and settlement system by agreement with the creditor or seller", "(E) Provides prepaid access", or "(F) Accepts and transmits funds only integral to the sale of goods or the provision of services, other than money transmission services, by the person who is accepting and transmitting the funds" — [31 CFR 1010.100](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/31/1010.100).
- FinCEN's 2019 guidance applies these definitions to convertible virtual currency business models — [FinCEN FIN-2019-G001](https://www.fincen.gov/sites/default/files/2019-05/FinCEN%20Guidance%20CVC%20FINAL%20508.pdf). It was not re-read for this note.

#### States
- The CSBS model act (MTMA) excludes closed-loop stored value, meaning value redeemable only by the issuer for goods or services of the issuer or its affiliates. The rationale is that closed-loop systems involve only two parties — [CSBS activities and exemptions template](https://www.csbs.org/sites/default/files/2019-10/Activities%20and%20Exemptions%20Templates.pdf); [CSBS MTMA](https://www.csbs.org/sites/default/files/2023-02/CSBS%20Money%20Transmission%20Modernization%20Act.pdf). State versions include Wisconsin (adds franchisees) and Minnesota — [Wis. Stat. 217.02](https://docs.legis.wisconsin.gov/statutes/statutes/217/02/8?up=1); [Minn. Stat. 53B](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/53B/pdf). Colorado issued interim guidance on crypto under its MTMA on 2025-12-18 — [Colorado Division of Banking](https://banking.colorado.gov/sites/banking/files/documents/Interim_Guidance_Cryptocurrency_and_the_MTMA_Final_12.18.25.pdf).

#### Sanctions (OFAC)
- "OFAC may impose civil penalties for sanctions violations generally based on a strict liability legal standard". OFAC advises virtual currency companies to "Incorporate geolocation tools and IP address blocking controls", including screening against known VPN IP addresses, and gives an enforcement example in which a company tracked IPs for security but did not use them for sanctions screening — [OFAC Sanctions Compliance Guidance for the Virtual Currency Industry, Oct 2021](https://ofac.treasury.gov/media/913571/download?inline).
- With gamma, URnetwork's TAO goes into a protocol pool and the alpha is burned, so there is no recipient account. Provider miners are paid by protocol emissions, not by the payer — [gamma guide](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/docs/guides/gamma.mdx). Gamma deposits are public chain storage that names the funding coldkey — [internal] report.

#### EU (MiCA)
- Article 4(3) exempts offers of crypto-assets usable "only... in exchange for goods and services within a limited network of merchants that have contractual arrangements with the offeror" from white-paper obligations. Under Article 4(4) the exemption falls away if admission to trading on an EU platform is sought — [ESMA single rulebook, MiCA Art. 4](https://www.esma.europa.eu/publications-and-data/interactive-single-rulebook/mica/article-4-offers-public-crypto-assets-other); [Bank of Latvia classification guidance](https://bank.lv/en/operational-areas/licensing/crypto-asset/classification-of-crypto-assets).

#### Apple App Store
- 3.1.1: "If you want to unlock features or functionality within your app, (by way of example: subscriptions, in-game currencies...), you must use in-app purchase. Apps may not use their own mechanisms to unlock content or functionality, such as license keys... cryptocurrencies and cryptocurrency wallets". "Any credits or in-game currencies purchased via in-app purchase may not expire" — [App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/).
- 3.1.1(a) and 3.1.3: entitlements "are not required for developers to include buttons, external links, or other calls to action in their United States storefront apps". 3.1.3 exempts "apps on the United States storefront" from the ban on encouraging non-IAP purchase. 3.1.3(e) covers physical goods and services "consumed outside of the app". 3.1.3(f) exempts free companion apps for paid web VoIP, cloud storage, email or web hosting with no purchase calls to action. 3.1.5 says crypto wallets need organization developers, and exchanges need licensing — [App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/).
- **Ninth Circuit (Dec 2025):** it affirmed contempt and found the 27% link-out commission prohibitive, but held that "neither the text nor the spirit of the injunction prohibits commissions outright". It remanded for a non-prohibitive fee covering "genuinely and reasonably necessary" coordination costs, which must be approved before Apple charges it. Apple may stop external links from being more prominent than IAP — [SCL LLP, 2025-12-29](https://scl-llp.com/ninth-circuit-upholds-apple-contempt-finding-but-narrows-scope-of-remedial-relief/); [Fenwick](https://www.fenwick.com/insights/publications/ninth-circuit-largely-upholds-ruling-in-epic-v-apple).

Secondary sources say the Court granted it on about June 30 or July 2, 2026, limited to the contempt "spirit" question, with remand rate proceedings continuing — [cert petition](https://www.supremecourt.gov/DocketPDF/25/25-1311/409561/20260526163506450_2026-05-26%20Apple-Epic%20--%20Cert%20Petition%20and%20Appendix.pdf); [Oyez](https://www.oyez.org/cases/2026/25-1311); [MacDailyNews, 2026-08-14](https://macdailynews.com/2026/08/14/u-s-supreme-court-clears-path-for-app-store-commission-showdown-as-apple-must-defend-its-rates-in-lower-court/).

#### Google Play (US)
- "Google will not require the use of Google Play Billing in apps distributed on the Google Play Store" for US users (changes effective 2025-10-29). ### Inferences
- **Closed-loop design rules**, which keep the balance inside (kkk) and (ff)(4)(iii)(A) and the (ff)(5)(ii)(F) exclusion:
  - URnetwork is the merchant of record selling *its own* service. Chutes, Targon and the others are subprocessors that URnetwork pays from its own treasury.
  - No cash-out, no user-to-user transfers, and no user-directed payments to named third parties.
  - Cap loads at ≤$2,000 per user per day, and block sales over $10,000 per person per day.
  - Refunds go only to the original payment method.
- Never let users hold or redeem gamma. Gamma stays URnetwork's internal procurement instrument.
- Because gamma and provider balances cannot be refunded, user refunds (card chargebacks, store refunds) must come from URnetwork's fiat treasury. Provider prepayments are unrecoverable, which argues for just-in-time minting.
- **App stores:**
  - iOS outside the US: compute credits are digital services used in the app, so they need IAP. IAP credits may not expire, so balances bought by IAP cannot carry an expiry.
  - iOS US storefront: a "buy credits on the web" link is allowed, with no approved Apple fee as of the remand.
  - Android in the US: alternative billing or link-out is allowed, but fees apply from 2026-10-01.
  - Paying with crypto *inside* the app to unlock compute conflicts with 3.1.1 outside the US.
- Sanctions: card and app-store rails already screen. URnetwork should add IP/geo-blocking for sanctioned jurisdictions (OFAC's VPN-IP point bears directly on a VPN company). It should also screen the provider entities it pays in TAO. Gamma mints have no payee, but the providers delivering the service, and their miners, are still the counterparties to diligence.

- Export-control and data-residency implications of running TEE workloads on anonymous miners' hardware were not researched.
