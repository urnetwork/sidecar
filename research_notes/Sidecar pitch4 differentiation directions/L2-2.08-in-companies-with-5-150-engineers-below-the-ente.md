Conventions and method:
- **[opened]**: read on 1 Nov 2026. Cursor forum topics were read in full through Discourse's JSON API (`forum.cursor.com/t/{id}.json`, `search.json`). ## 1. Is there public demand below Enterprise for evidence of what agents did and reached? How much, and of what kind?

### Takeaway
There is real demand below Enterprise, but it is mostly for **attribution of usage, cost and AI-written code to people, projects and clients**. That is the data an engineering leader needs to justify and allocate AI spend. It is not demand for audit evidence.

On pages I opened, I counted distinct people below or around the Enterprise line:

| Kind of request | People | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Usage, identity or AI-code attribution | **43** | 11 on two Cursor threads, 32 on Claude Code issues. At least 14 say they are on a Team/Teams plan. |
| Compliance documents or controls below Enterprise | **6** | DPA, governance admin, GxP, a client AI policy |
| Actor attribution (which human or agent acted) | **6** | |
| Approval or audit records | **3** | Plan unstated |
| Per-connection reach receipts | **0** | |

Workaround tools for audit get almost no adoption. Four Cursor forum tool posts drew 0 likes, and five GitHub repositories have at most 2 stars. Usage trackers get thousands of stars (ccusage has 19,286).

There is also a counter-signal: 20 claude-code issues (68 thumbs-up) ask Claude Code to stop writing attribution trailers into commits.

### Inferences
- **The below-Enterprise ask is attribution for management, not evidence for auditors.**
  - Of 58 counted people (43 + 6 + 6 + 3), 43 ask who used what, on which project or client, at what cost, and how much code AI wrote.
  - The strongest voices tie it to ROI ("prove the clear ROI … to our management") or cost pass-through to clients.
  - Only 3 ask for approval or audit records, and no one below Enterprise asks for receipts for network reach.
- **Team buyers are already building evidence pipelines themselves.**
  - Team-plan orgs run their own OTel collectors (ClickHouse, Azure Monitor, Jellyfish) and dashboards, and they hit identity gaps (54% unattributed rows).
  - That is a revealed preference for an attribution layer the vendor does not provide at Team. It is also a cost: each harness needs its own pipeline.
- **"Audit without Enterprise" is a smaller wedge than 1.10 assumed.**
  - The wall is lower than it looks: Claude's self-serve Enterprise is a card purchase from 20 seats, and Copilot Business logs GitHub-side agent activity with session IDs.
  - The people asking for audit-grade records mostly speak in Enterprise contexts: the existing Enterprise audit stream (156247, prior round), NERC CIP in #84504, and evaluators of Enterprise OTel.
  - My reading of the evidence is that the gap is real mainly for:
    - Claude teams under 20 seats;
    - Cursor and Devin Teams orgs (up to ~200 users on Devin);
    - agencies and outsourcers.
- **Developers resist commit attribution they did not choose but accept accurate attribution.** That supports keeping attribution in the record, with trailers generated only on request (consistent with 1.1's direction).

## 2. Do customers' questionnaires or auditors ask these companies for agent records?

### Takeaway
**Auditors and consultants treat AI-written code as ordinary SOC 2 change management.** They ask for three things:
- an inventory of AI tools;
- an approval process for tools;
- evidence of human review.

Forge evidence such as protected branches and PR approvals already supplies most of this.

**Agent-run records appear only as stronger asks**, from a vendor (CrashOverride: Type 2 records of "AI agent version, reviewer name, review date, approval") and from one CPA firm, for agents generally (Accorp: logs that "attribute each agent action to the session, user, and workflow that initiated it"). Linford & Co, another CPA firm, says auditors are "asking about" shadow AI, code-completion tools included, but lists no agent-log evidence. I found no coding-agent evidence guidance from Vanta, Drata, Secureframe, A-LIGN or Prescient.

**First-hand client asks at small firms** come from three directions:
- agencies whose clients restrict AI by contract;
- outsourcers whose clients want per-task cost logs;
- startups selling to finance and legal customers.

Security reviews in these threads target the AI tool itself at purchase (its SOC 2 report or DPA), not the team's agent records.

### Inferences
- **What small companies are asked today is answerable without agent records.** They are asked:
  - whether they use AI tools and which ones;

  - for human review of AI-written code;
  - for the AI vendor's own SOC 2 report or DPA.

  A PR-approval trail and a policy satisfy most of this. Agent records become necessary when:
  1. an auditor samples AI-authored changes (CrashOverride's Type 2 list);

- **Agencies and outsourcers are the clearest small-company segment with a client-driven need.** Their clients prohibit AI per contract, or want per-task costs passed through. These map to per-client policy enforcement plus a per-client record, more than to SOC 2.

## 3. Would 5–150-engineer companies pay a Team price for a signed export that covers every harness?

### Takeaway
The evidence supports paying for **cross-harness attribution data that proves ROI and allocates cost**:
- Teams-plan customers ask for Enterprise-gated analytics "without Enterprise-scale budgets".
- They build their own OTel pipelines.
- They already pay engineering-intelligence vendors (Jellyfish, DX, Span) that ingest several coding tools.

The evidence does **not** show willingness to pay for a signed audit export below Enterprise:
- I found no first-hand compliance trigger.
- Audit workaround tools have near-zero adoption.
- Two walls are lower than they look: Claude's Enterprise is a card purchase from 20 seats, and Copilot Business already logs GitHub-side agent sessions.

**Multi-harness use** is common in small companies, and every vendor's analytics stop at its own harness. Coverage of every harness is therefore the most defensible part of the offer, but the buyer values it as one view of all AI-tool usage, not as an audit artifact.

## 5. What this means for pitch4: lead buyer, the "audit without Enterprise" wedge, reach receipts versus device and tag logs, and card versus annual

### Takeaway
- **Lead with the developer and the engineering leader, not the assurance buyer.** Sell Team, on a card, as **cross-harness attribution**: who or which agent, which repo, client or task, which model, what it cost, what AI wrote, and who approved. Make "records an auditor can check" the by-product, not the headline.
- **Narrow "audit without Enterprise" to the segments where it bites:**
  - Claude teams under 20 seats;
  - Cursor and Devin Teams orgs;
  - agencies and outsourcers with client AI terms.
- **Reach receipts set the sidecar apart from Tailscale's and Devin Connect's device, tag and address logs only if each connection carries actor attribution:** initiator, approver, credential owner, run and repo. The visible demand is in identity failures, not in requests for network logs.

### Cited Findings
- **Network logs attribute to infrastructure, not people or runs:**
  - Tailscale flow logs (Premium/Enterprise) and Devin Connect gateway logs attribute to a device, tag or "client address" (1.3) — [Tailscale blog](https://tailscale.com/blog/codex-cloud-tailscale); [Devin Connect](https://docs.devin.ai/enterprise/deployment/devin-connect) [opened in 1.3]
  - Bedrock CloudTrail behind Claude's gateway shows "the task role ARN … no per-user attribution" — [issue #84504](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/issues/84504) [opened]
- **Identity failures that a run-level record would expose or prevent:**
  - Cursor team follow-ups execute "as user A" — [158866](https://forum.cursor.com/t/cursor-cloud-agent-sessions-are-shared-across-users-act-on-behalf/158866)
  - Cursor PR authorship ties attribution to review governance (no self-approval) — [168503](https://forum.cursor.com/t/cloud-agent-pr-attribution-gh-account-wide-app-vs-personal-oauth-confusion/168503)
  - GitHub users ask for agent identities linked to owners — [discussion #203213](https://github.com/orgs/community/discussions/203213)
  - Claude cloud sessions lose identity (54% of rows unattributed) — [#90288](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/issues/90288)
  - All [opened]
- **Below-Enterprise demand is for attribution:** 43 people asked for attribution, against 3 for approval records and 0 for reach receipts (Q1).
- **Card versus annual:** Team plans are card-bought, and invoicing is an Enterprise feature (Q4).
- **Segments with client-driven needs:** agency (136492) and outsourcer (160895), alongside AT&T SAIR's per-instance approval and SBOM terms (1.8) — [136492](https://forum.cursor.com/t/repository-level-ai-policy-file-ai-policy-txt-for-client-compliance/136492); [160895](https://forum.cursor.com/t/chat-agent-session-metadata-for-client-task-and-cost-attribution/160895) [opened]

### Inferences
- **Pitch4's "Admins answer" reader should be reframed** from "admins answer auditors" to "engineering leaders answer: who used which agent on what, at what cost, with what result". Auditor answers come from the same record. This keeps "one write, three readers" and matches the 43-to-3 ratio of demand.
- **"Audit without Enterprise" should be a proof point inside Team, not the wedge.** The segments where it bites are small or specific: under 20 Claude seats, Cursor and Devin Teams orgs, and agencies. Larger teams can buy Claude Enterprise by card at 20 seats.
- **Reach receipts need actor attribution to stand apart.** Each receipt should carry who initiated the run, whose credential was used, who approved, the run and repo, and the model. Device or tag logs (Tailscale, Devin Connect) and role-level CloudTrail cannot provide this without harness context. That is a differentiation, but there is no evidence yet that buyers below Enterprise will pay for it on its own.
- **Commit-trailer backlash argues for attribution held in the record, with "verified" trailers or notes generated on request.** It also argues against stamping commits by default. Issue #104719 asks for "verified commit attribution" parity — [issue #104719](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/issues/104719) [title seen in API search; not opened].
- **Export to where Team buyers already look.** That means OTLP, engineering-intelligence tools (Jellyfish, DX, Span) and GRC platforms (Vanta, Drata), with identity filled in across harnesses. This is a cheaper route to market than asking a 5–150-engineer company to buy a new audit console.

### Gaps
- No direct evidence that buyers below Enterprise would pay extra for per-connection receipts.
- No measured willingness to pay for cross-harness attribution versus Jellyfish, DX or Span.
- No data on how many agencies and outsourcers face client AI clauses.
