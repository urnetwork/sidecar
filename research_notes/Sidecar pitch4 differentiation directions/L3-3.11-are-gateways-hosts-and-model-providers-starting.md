## 1. What has each actor shipped or announced, with dates, and does any of it give the customer per-request proof of what served the call?

### Takeaway
Per-request proof exists only at two small attested hosts: **Phala** (signed ACI receipts) and **NEAR AI** (response signatures).

No gateway gives customers cryptographic per-request proof. That covers OpenRouter, Kilo Gateway, OpenCode Zen and Go, Cline Enterprise, LiteLLM, Coder and Prisma AIRS. Anthropic does not either.

OpenRouter is moving the other way. Between 21 Sep and 29 Oct 2026 it formalised customer and provider **declarations** (zero data retention, region, quantization) that it "records ... and routes on ... and does not independently verify". Its attestation field stays empty, and none of the 85 API changelog entries since 3 Jul 2026 mentions attestation.

The one new party keeping receipts is Phala's own open-source client. Its Private AI Proxy desktop app and `pap` CLI, from 0.2.0 on 28 Sep 2026, audit every response and keep the signed receipt in a local database. It does this for Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Pi and Hermes.

#### OpenRouter
- **What "attestation" means in its docs: customer declarations.**
  - The 4.8 MB docs dump uses "attest" only for customer declarations. It contains no "enclave", no "confidential computing" or "confidential inference", and no standalone "TEE". "Receipt" appears twice, neither time about inference (a Stripe receipt email and a classification example) [computed].
  - Private endpoints: "A declaration is your attestation about your agreement with the upstream. OpenRouter records it and routes on it, and does not independently verify the upstream's contractual behavior."
  - On the audit trail: "The difference is what your audit trail records: **Declared non-ZDR** is known and attested, **Not declared** is unknown."
  - On BYOK keys: "The declaration is your attestation about where the provider processes requests made with your own account ... OpenRouter does not verify it."
  - The `declared_zdr` field: "Attest that this deployment retains no prompt or completion data."
  - Source: [OpenRouter docs, full text](https://openrouter.ai/docs/llms-full.txt); [ZDR guide](https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/features/zdr) [verbatim].
- **Private endpoints and private providers: routing to a team's own deployments, unverified.**
  - "A private endpoint connects a catalog model to a deployment you operate in your own provider account, authenticated with your BYOK key."
  - Setup: "Enter the HTTPS base URL of your endpoint and the upstream model or deployment ID it serves."
  - For private providers: "OpenRouter does not verify the upstream's actual data handling."
  - Source: [Private endpoints guide](https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/routing/private-endpoints) [verbatim, via llms-full].
- **Changelog dating** [computed].
  - The API changelog is "generated automatically from [OpenAPI specification] diffs when releases ship". Its 85 entries run from 3 Jul to 29 Oct 2026.
  - Dates of first appearance:
    - `declared_zdr`: 21 Sep 2026.
    - `POST /private-endpoints` (new): 28 Sep.
    - `declared_region`: 6 Oct.
    - `POST /private-providers` and the `PrivateEndpointQuantization` schema: 29 Oct.
    - Quantization schema changes: from 27 Jul to 29 Oct.
  - **No entry mentions attestation or Exacto.**
  - Source: [OpenRouter API changelog](https://openrouter.ai/docs/changelog) [verbatim].
- **Quantization is self-declared.** The provider-facing model listing (schema "2.4") reads `"quantization": "fp8", // or null when undeclared` — [OpenRouter docs](https://openrouter.ai/docs/llms-full.txt) [verbatim].
- **Per-request records exist, but are unsigned.**
  - Logs show "each generation as it happened: which model and provider served it, how long it took, what it cost, and, if you have enabled Input & Output Logging, the exact prompt and completion."
  - The Generations tab gives "One row per completed generation, with date, model, final provider, generation ID, status, attempts, API key, and latency."
  - `GET /api/v1/generation` returns "model, provider, tokens, cost, latency, and finish reason".
  - Activity Export is "aggregated usage data as CSV or PDF". Broadcast sends traces to the customer's observability tools.
  - No retention period is stated on the Logs page.
  - Sources: [Logs](https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/features/logs); [Activity Export](https://openrouter.ai/docs/cookbook/administration/activity-export) [verbatim].
- **The attestation field is present but dormant.**
  - The endpoint JSON for GLM 5.3 (49 endpoints, 1 Nov 2026) carries `required_attestation_types: []` at model level and `requiredAttestationTypes: []` in every `provider_info`. The public `all-providers` list (96 providers) has no attestation key [computed].
  - A third-party survey of OpenRouter's model fields (`deankerr/orca`, data from 23-24 Oct 2026) marks `required_attestation_types` with 💤. That is the same mark it gives fields that are "null in every sample".
  - No OpenRouter-authored code on GitHub uses the field (`gh search code`, 1 Nov 2026).
  - Sources: [model-endpoints API](https://openrouter.ai/api/frontend/v1/catalog/model-endpoints?permaslug=z-ai/glm-5.3-20260816); [all-providers](https://openrouter.ai/api/frontend/v1/all-providers); [orca field notes](https://github.com/deankerr/orca/blob/HEAD/docs/openrouter/models.md).

  - OpenRouter's Phala and NEAR AI provider pages and its Enterprise page contain zero occurrences of "TEE", "confidential", "attest", "enclave", "receipt" or "verif". None of the 452 models in `/api/v1/models` is labelled TEE or confidential.
  - Sources: [all-providers](https://openrouter.ai/api/frontend/v1/all-providers); [Phala provider page](https://openrouter.ai/provider/phala); [NEAR AI provider page](https://openrouter.ai/provider/near-ai); [Enterprise](https://openrouter.ai/enterprise); [models API](https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models) [computed].

#### Attested hosts and verifiers
- **Phala and ACI: what a receipt is.**
  - "A receipt is a signed, per-request event log. It binds the request bytes the workload received, the bytes it forwarded, the upstream verification outcome, and the response bytes it returned — all hashed inside the TEE and signed with an attested receipt key."
  - "`model` is the model id the client asked for." Aggregator receipts add `upstream.verified` with `"model_id": "<upstream model served>"` and a `session_id`.
  - Source: [ACI spec §7](https://github.com/Dstack-TEE/private-ai-gateway/blob/HEAD/spec/aci.md) [verbatim].
- **Status of the ACI reference implementation.**
  - "This repository contains the Rust reference implementation of Attested Confidential Inference (ACI) ... It is a developer preview."
  - Repo facts: Apache-2.0, 44 stars, created 19 May 2026, pushed 31 Oct 2026 [GitHub API]. The spec has 15 commits in this repo since 3 Jul 2026, the latest on 29 Oct.
  - Source: [README](https://github.com/Dstack-TEE/private-ai-gateway/blob/HEAD/README.md) [verbatim].
- **Phala's production docs.**
  - "This is the canonical per-response proof."
  - Fetching a receipt: "Use the same API key that made the inference request; a different key returns `404`".
  - The receipt events also include `route.selected` and `response.received`.
  - Sources: [Phala, Get Receipt](https://docs.phala.com/phala-cloud/confidential-ai/confidential-model/api-reference/receipts); [Receipts and Sessions](https://docs.phala.com/phala-cloud/confidential-ai/confidential-model/receipts-and-sessions) [verbatim].
- **Phala's own client keeps receipts (new).** Private AI Proxy desktop 0.2.0, 28 Sep 2026:
  - "**Agents.** Codex 0.157.1 ..., Claude Code 2.1.242+, OpenCode, Pi, Oh My Pi, OpenClaw and Hermes".
  - "**Receipts.** Every delivered response is audited after delivery; request details show the raw signed receipt, and a failed audit names the check that failed."
  - "**Fail closed, everywhere.**" Yet also: "Receipt audits report after delivery and never block a response."
  - Source: [desktop CHANGELOG](https://github.com/Dstack-TEE/private-ai-gateway/blob/HEAD/apps/desktop/CHANGELOG.md) [verbatim].
- **How the `pap` CLI stores receipts.**
  - "`usage show <id> --receipt` prints the signed receipt document (ACI spec §7.2) ... to archive or re-verify offline with `pap audit --receipt`."
  - "They stay in the local usage database, and `usage clear` deletes them with the records."
  - The usage database is SQLite in a private app-data file, with rows carrying `session_id`, `agent`, `model`, `path`, `status` and `streamed` [code].
  - Sources: [pap CLI docs](https://github.com/Dstack-TEE/private-ai-gateway/blob/HEAD/apps/desktop/docs/cli.md) [verbatim]; [usage.rs](https://github.com/Dstack-TEE/private-ai-gateway/blob/HEAD/apps/desktop/runtime/src/usage.rs) [code].
- **NEAR AI Cloud response signatures.**
  - Two signers: "Response signatures returned through the NEAR AI Cloud gateway can be produced by either a model-serving TEE or the gateway itself."
  - Payloads: `provider_tee` signs `<MODEL_ID>:<request_hash>:<response_hash>`; `gateway` signs `<request_hash>:<response_hash>`.
  - "Only `provider_tee` binds a response to a model-serving TEE. A `gateway` signature binds the response to the gateway, not to a model report."
  - When the gateway signs: "The Gateway signs a response itself when it changes the bytes it relays: by default on streamed completions (it removes per-chunk usage data), when it resolves a model alias, and on the Responses API."
  - Key sharing: "Instances of the same model share one signing key".
  - Chain gap: "Preflight Gateway and model evidence plus one response signature do not yet form a complete model-to-Gateway-to-final-response chain."
  - Source: [NEAR, Response Signatures](https://docs.near.ai/cloud/verification/cloud-api/response-signatures.md) [verbatim].
- **NEAR's verifier repos are small.**
  - `nearai/nearai-cloud-verifier`: MIT, 1 star, last push 2 Sep 2026.
  - `near-examples/nearai-cloud-verification-example`: MIT, 4 stars, last push 16 Sep 2026 [GitHub API].
  - The example documents both payload formats: "provider_tee: "{model_id}:{request_hash}:{response_hash}" (signed inside the model TEE)" and "gateway: "{request_hash}:{response_hash}" (signed inside the gateway TEE)".
  - Sources: [verification-helpers.js](https://github.com/near-examples/nearai-cloud-verification-example/blob/HEAD/utils/verification-helpers.js); [chat_verifier.py](https://github.com/nearai/nearai-cloud-verifier/blob/HEAD/py/chat_verifier.py) [code].
- **Tinfoil: no per-request receipts.**
  - "receipt" occurs 0 times in its 768 KB docs dump. The only per-request artefact is the `X-Tinfoil-Usage-Metrics` billing summary [computed].
  - Verification happens per connection against Sigstore. On direct access: "Attestation evidence is recorded in certificate transparency logs, but this does not protect the connection before data is sent, and Tinfoil does not provide supported tooling to reconstruct audit-time verification from those records."
  - Source: [Tinfoil docs](https://docs.tinfoil.sh/llms-full.txt) [verbatim].
- **Chutes: per-deployment evidence and catalog flags.**
  - "Chutes exposes confidential-compute metadata in the live model catalog. Use the boolean field, attach the verification date, and do not upgrade a parsed evidence response into a cryptographic attestation claim unless DCAP validation actually ran."
  - "Evidence endpoints are authenticated and chute-specific" (`GET /chutes/{id}/evidence?nonce=`).
  - "10 of them report `confidential_compute: true`" (17 models on 1 Nov 2026).
  - Sources: [Chutes private inference](https://chutes.ai/agents/private); [Chutes llms.txt](https://chutes.ai/llms.txt) [verbatim].
- **teep: verifies channels, not responses.**
  - Repo facts: AGPL-3.0, 29 stars, created 20 Mar 2026, pushed 1 Nov 2026 [GitHub API].
  - Its Go source never calls `/v1/signature`, `/v1/aci/receipts` or `X-Receipt-Id` [code, grep of `internal/`].
  - Its capture package "provides HTTP traffic recording and replay for attestation verification". It saves attestation exchanges, not per-response receipts.
  - Its own gap analysis credits NEAR's model-TEE signature as "the backend-authenticated receipt missing from Tinfoil cloud routing".
  - Sources: [teep capture.go](https://github.com/13rac1/teep/blob/HEAD/internal/capture/capture.go); [teep, Tinfoil cloud integrity](https://github.com/13rac1/teep/blob/HEAD/docs/attestation_gaps/tinfoil_cloud_integrity.md) [code].

## 2. Does any receipt bind the weights, quantization and runtime?

### Takeaway
No per-request receipt binds the served weights.
- **Phala:** its own reference gateway leaves `model_weights_provenance` "Unknown" for every provider.
- **NEAR:** binds the model ID string plus the measured compose file and image digests (the runtime), not weight hashes.
- **Tinfoil:** the only host that binds weights cryptographically (a dm-verity root of the files, plus the Hugging Face commit). It also binds the runtime (image digest and command). But it does so per deployment, not per request, and it issues no receipts.
- **Chutes:** binds "the specified Hugging Face model name and exact revision hash" into per-token proofs, but only validators check them. Clients see no weight hashes.

Quantization is declared everywhere and bound nowhere as a field: OpenRouter providers declare it, Chutes "would explicitly declare" it, Tinfoil lists it on model pages. At best it is bound implicitly through the digest of a quantized checkpoint (Tinfoil).

### Cited Findings
- **ACI has a weights claim, and nothing fills it.**
  - The claim: "`model_weights_provenance` | The served weights match their claimed provenance."
  - Claim sources: `"hardware_proven" | "verifier_derived" | "provider_asserted" | "operator_asserted"`. "Missing evidence is `unknown`: not a pass, not a refutation."
  - ACI v1 does not try to define "canonical model identifiers".
  - Source: [ACI spec §8 and Appendix B](https://github.com/Dstack-TEE/private-ai-gateway/blob/HEAD/spec/aci.md) [verbatim].
- **The reference gateway leaves weights unknown.**
  - The Intel TDX claim mapper, used for dstack and Chutes, says: "// model_weights_provenance stays Unknown: no verifier here checks the // served weights."
  - That comment is the claim's only non-test mention in `claims.rs`. Tests assert it is `Unknown`. The other mappers (Tinfoil, Secret AI, Armet AI, Generic) never set it.
  - The runtime is partly covered: "OS provenance from the attested image hash".
  - Source: [claims.rs](https://github.com/Dstack-TEE/private-ai-gateway/blob/HEAD/src/aggregator/service/claims.rs) [code].
- **Phala's docs list four receipt proofs, none about weights.**
  1. The signature is by an attested keyset key.
  2. `workload_keyset_digest` matches the attestation report.
  3. Exact request and response bytes match their hashes.
  4. The required upstream verification result is recorded.
  - Source: [Phala, Receipts and Sessions](https://docs.phala.com/phala-cloud/confidential-ai/confidential-model/receipts-and-sessions) [verbatim].
- **NEAR binds the runtime configuration, not the weights.**
  - "`info.tcb_info.app_compose` | Raw measured configuration string. Hash it and match it to the quote's MRCONFIGID."
  - "Image provenance is separate from hardware attestation. A verified quote can bind a configuration measurement to a runtime. Provenance determines whether the image digests in that configuration were built by a source and build process your policy accepts."
  - Neither page mentions weight hashes.
  - Sources: [NEAR Model Attestations](https://docs.near.ai/cloud/verification/cloud-api/model-attestations.md); [Image Provenance](https://docs.near.ai/cloud/verification/cloud-api/image-provenance.md) [verbatim].
- **teep's per-provider weights verdicts.**
  - Its factor `measured_model_weights` means "Attestation includes hashes of model weight files. Without this, a compromised provider could load a backdoored model."
  - Tinfoil: "`measured_model_weights` | `enforced` (transitive) | Model weights attested via dm-verity + Sigstore chain".
  - NEAR Cloud, NEAR Direct and Chutes all carry `measured_model_weights` in their default allow-fail lists.
  - Chutes: "Teep returns `Fail` for `measured_model_weights` with detail "no model weight hashes" because neither weight verification mechanism exposes verifiable data to clients."
  - Venice behind the ACI gateway fails and waives it too.
  - Sources: [README_ADVANCED](https://github.com/13rac1/teep/blob/HEAD/README_ADVANCED.md); [Tinfoil support](https://github.com/13rac1/teep/blob/HEAD/docs/providers/tinfoil/tinfoil_support.md); [supply-chain map](https://github.com/13rac1/teep/blob/HEAD/docs/plans/supply_chain_attestation_map.md); [sek8s integrity](https://github.com/13rac1/teep/blob/HEAD/docs/attestation_gaps/sek8s_integrity.md); [Venice ACI gateway](https://github.com/13rac1/teep/blob/HEAD/docs/attestation_gaps/venice_aci_gateway.md) [code].
- **What a Tinfoil config pins.**
  - The weights: `repo: "google/gemma-4-31B-it@419b2efe421994fdfd3394e621983d4cc511cd4f"` and `mpk: "0900ca6b...e1276ab_62578683904_..."`.
  - The runtime: `image: "ghcr.io/myorg/vllm-gemma4@sha256:<digest>"` and `command: ["--model", "/tinfoil/mpk/mpk-0900ca6b...", ...]`.
  - "Tinfoil measures your config at each tag and publishes the measurement to the Sigstore transparency log". "The `image` field must include a SHA256 digest".
  - A Moonshot AI model page discloses "**Quantization:** MXFP4 weights / MXFP8 activations (native quantization-aware release from Moonshot AI)".
  - Source: [Tinfoil docs](https://docs.tinfoil.sh/llms-full.txt) [verbatim].
- **Chutes binds model identity only inside its validator network.**
  - "cllmv ... integrates with the SGLang inference engine to provide per-token verification hashes. Crucially, the specified Hugging Face model name and exact revision hash are cryptographically bound into the per-token proofs."
  - Quantization and runtime by declaration: "Chutes almost never uses quantized models, and if so, it would be explicitly declared." "You can see the exact engine_args and source code."
  - Source: [Chutes security architecture](https://chutes.ai/docs/core-concepts/security-architecture) [verbatim; vendor claims].
- **Gateways bind no weights.**
  - LiteLLM: "an opaque deployment hash" ([#35246](https://github.com/BerriAI/litellm/issues/35246)).
  - Coder records provider and model ([audit](https://coder.com/docs/ai-coder/ai-gateway/audit)).
  - OpenRouter quantization is "null when undeclared" ([docs](https://openrouter.ai/docs/llms-full.txt)).
- **Anthropic's weights check is internal only.** "checks that outputs come from a specific set of model weights" — [roadmap](https://www.anthropic.com/responsible-scaling-policy/roadmap).

### Inferences
- **The weights slot in per-request receipts is empty everywhere, and ACI already defines it.**
  - A model-server sidecar that signs a manifest per request (weight-file digests, quantization, engine and version, parser and template hash) can fill `model_weights_provenance`, with `source: "operator_asserted"`, or `"hardware_proven"` when it runs in a TEE with a dm-verity-style root.
  - That needs no new format, so it sharpens 2.03's "propose a manifest spec" direction.
- **The precise claim pitch4 can make:** "per-request receipts that name the weights, quantization and engine". Not "only we prove weights".
  - Tinfoil already proves weights per deployment, with hardware roots, which is stronger evidence than an operator-signed receipt.
- **Streaming matters for coding agents.** NEAR signs streamed completions at the gateway by default, so the model TEE is not bound for the main coding-agent traffic. The ACI reference gives no receipt for failed streaming requests.

### Gaps
- Whether Phala's production `inference.phala.com` deploys any weights verifier beyond the reference code is not published.
- Whether NEAR's measured `app_compose` pins a Hugging Face revision or weight digest could not be checked without fetching a live report.
- Whether Tinfoil's quantization flags appear in measured config (rather than in the checkpoint itself) varies by model and was not checked repo by repo.

## 3. How long are receipts kept, and can customers export them and re-verify them offline?

### Takeaway
Hosts keep receipts briefly and tell the client to keep them.
- **ACI:** the spec says receipts are "retained for a bounded, implementation-defined period" and "Clients SHOULD fetch receipts promptly". The reference gateway keeps them **3,600 seconds, in memory, lost on restart**. Phala's production setting is not published.
- **NEAR:** states no retention period, only a "short, bounded" retry window while a signature "may still be recorded". It tells clients to retain the exact bytes and the verified preflight reports.
- **Tinfoil:** keeps deployment measurements permanently in Sigstore, but no request records. It offers no "supported tooling to reconstruct audit-time verification".
- **Chutes:** lists validator audit reports "from the past week".

Offline re-verification is designed in. An ACI receipt is "one self-contained file a client can archive and re-verify offline", and NEAR signatures verify offline from retained bytes. But only if someone archived the evidence at the time.

Today that someone is Phala's own desktop client, locally and per developer, since 28 Sep 2026. teep does not keep receipts, OpenRouter does not relay them, and the gateways do not store them.

### Cited Findings
- **ACI spec on retention and offline checks.**
  - "Receipts are retained for a bounded, implementation-defined period. Clients SHOULD fetch receipts promptly. An unknown or expired id returns `not_found`."
  - "Any JSON encoding of the same document verifies, and the whole receipt is one self-contained file a client can archive and re-verify offline."
  - Sessions: "A session MUST remain retrievable, unchanged, for as long as the service still serves any receipt citing it (§7.1), and SHOULD remain available longer for receipts clients have archived."
  - Source: [ACI spec §7.1, §7.2, §8](https://github.com/Dstack-TEE/private-ai-gateway/blob/HEAD/spec/aci.md) [verbatim].
- **Reference gateway retention.**
  - "`receipt_ttl_seconds` remains a service-library setting with default `3600`; it is not a static gateway configuration field."
  - "**Receipts are kept only in memory.** A receipt is kept for `receipt_ttl_seconds` and is lost if the gateway restarts. The spec lets an implementation choose its retention period (§7.1), but a restart shortens it without warning. Attested sessions are stored on disk, survive restarts, and are kept as long as any receipt cites them."
  - "**Failed streaming requests get no receipt.**"
  - Sources: [configuration reference](https://github.com/Dstack-TEE/private-ai-gateway/blob/HEAD/docs/configuration-reference.md); [known conformance gaps](https://github.com/Dstack-TEE/private-ai-gateway/blob/HEAD/docs/reviews/aci-spec-conformance-gaps.md) [verbatim]; `receipt_ttl_seconds: 3600` default in [config.rs](https://github.com/Dstack-TEE/private-ai-gateway/blob/HEAD/src/aggregator/service/config.rs) [code].
- **Phala hands retention to the customer.** "Audit evidence | Attestation reports, receipts, sessions, and source provenance can be retained by the customer." And: "No body retention in receipts | Receipts store hashes and facts, not prompt or response text." — [Phala, Compliance and open source](https://docs.phala.com/phala-cloud/confidential-ai/confidential-model/compliance-and-open-source) (via [llms-full](https://docs.phala.com/llms-full.txt)) [verbatim].
- **Phala's client export and offline audit.**
  - Each receipt is saved "byte for byte as the service returned it, to archive or re-verify offline with `pap audit --receipt`". It stays "in the local usage database", and "`usage clear` deletes them".
  - "CSV export covers all records matching its filters". Receipts themselves are printed per record with `usage show <id> --receipt`.
  - Size cap: "An audit reads at most 64 KiB of a receipt".
  - Source: [pap CLI docs](https://github.com/Dstack-TEE/private-ai-gateway/blob/HEAD/apps/desktop/docs/cli.md) [verbatim].
- **NEAR on retention.**
  - "A `404` means no matching signature was found; it can be retried for a short, bounded interval when the signature may still be recorded."
  - "Build the request body once and retain the exact bytes sent by the client. Retain the raw response bytes before parsing JSON or Server-Sent Events."
  - "verify every candidate and retain the verified results". "Do not use a report obtained after the completion as a substitute for preflight evidence."
  - The reference page states no retention period [computed: no "retain", "expire", "minutes", "hours" or "days" in it].
  - Browser checking is available through the "Verification Center at cloud.near.ai/verify".
  - Sources: [Response Signatures](https://docs.near.ai/cloud/verification/cloud-api/response-signatures.md); [Model Attestations](https://docs.near.ai/cloud/verification/cloud-api/model-attestations.md); [Response Signature Verification](https://docs.near.ai/cloud/verification/reference/response-signature-verification.md); [Verification overview](https://docs.near.ai/cloud/verification.md) [verbatim].
- **Tinfoil retention.** Config measurements go to the public Sigstore log. "Tinfoil does not provide supported tooling to reconstruct audit-time verification from those records." There are no per-request receipts — [Tinfoil docs](https://docs.tinfoil.sh/llms-full.txt) [verbatim].
- **Chutes audit API.** "List Audit Entries List all audit reports from the past week." `GET /audit/download` serves "report data". `POST /audit/miner_data` takes miner signatures, so these are validator-network reports, not customer receipts — [Chutes Audit API](https://chutes.ai/docs/api-reference/audit) [verbatim].
- **teep keeps no per-response receipts.** It records "every request/response pair" of attestation traffic for replay ([capture.go](https://github.com/13rac1/teep/blob/HEAD/internal/capture/capture.go)) and never fetches receipts or signatures [code].
- **Gateways' own records.**

  - Cline: the customer bucket, with "retention policies based on your compliance requirements" ([Prompt Storage](https://docs.cline.bot/enterprise-solutions/monitoring/prompt-storage)).
  - Coder: 60 days by default (earlier round).
  - Kilo: admin events only ([Kilo docs](https://kilo.ai/docs/llms.txt)).

### Inferences
- **2.11's "keeping receipts under team keys" gap is real, with one correction.**
  - Keeping receipts *per developer* already exists for Phala's own service (`pap`, Apache-2.0, 28 Sep 2026). It is linked to agent and session fields, is local only, is deletable with `usage clear`, and is limited to ACI services.
  - Still unclaimed: team-keyed retention that outlives a laptop; one archive across hosts (ACI, NEAR, self-hosted servers); and links from each receipt to the agent session, change set and approval.
- **The archive has to be on the request path.**
  - ACI receipts expire in about an hour and vanish on restart.
  - NEAR needs the exact request and response bytes, including SSE framing, kept from the moment of the call.

- **Gateway logs are weak evidence even when kept.**

- NEAR's signature store window is unpublished.

- Whether OpenRouter keeps generation logs for a fixed period was not found.

## 4. What the answer means for pitch4's differentiation and product offering

### Takeaway
Verification has not become a commodity, but it is converging on an open stack that pitch4 should plug into rather than compete with:
- ACI receipts plus Phala's `pap` client;
- NEAR signatures;
- teep;
- Tinfoil per-deployment attestation.

pitch4's model-access promise should be reframed in three ways:
1. The model-server sidecar issues **per-request receipts that name the weights, quantization and engine**. No host's receipt does this today, and ACI's own weights claim sits "Unknown".
2. The sidecar **fetches and keeps every other party's receipt** within its short window, with the attestation it cites, under team keys and linked to the session. Today the host forgets it within an hour, or only a single developer's laptop keeps it.
3. Gateway routes (OpenRouter, Kilo, OpenCode) are recorded as **declared**, not proven.

pitch4 should also stop implying that nobody keeps receipts. Phala's client does, per developer.

### Cited Findings
- **pitch4 lines tested.** "Proves The sidecar beside the model server signs which weights it serves." "Agents reach the inference servers you run, such as vLLM, SGLang, Ollama, or llama.cpp, and nothing else." "Each is a scoped grant with a signed receipt." — [pitch4.html](/Users/brien/urnetwork/sidecar/pitch4.html) [verbatim, local file].
- **No per-request weights binding exists anywhere.** ACI `model_weights_provenance` "stays Unknown" ([claims.rs](https://github.com/Dstack-TEE/private-ai-gateway/blob/HEAD/src/aggregator/service/claims.rs)). NEAR covers compose and images ([NEAR](https://docs.near.ai/cloud/verification/cloud-api/image-provenance.md)). Only Tinfoil binds weights, per deployment ([teep, Tinfoil](https://github.com/13rac1/teep/blob/HEAD/docs/providers/tinfoil/tinfoil_support.md)).
- **Short provider retention, with the client expected to archive.** ACI reference: 3,600 s in memory, lost on restart ([conformance gaps](https://github.com/Dstack-TEE/private-ai-gateway/blob/HEAD/docs/reviews/aci-spec-conformance-gaps.md)). Phala: "can be retained by the customer" ([Phala compliance](https://docs.phala.com/phala-cloud/confidential-ai/confidential-model/compliance-and-open-source)).
- **The nearest existing product to pitch4's model access.**
  - Phala's `pap` desktop client: a local fail-closed proxy for "Claude Code 2.1.242+, OpenCode, Pi ... and Hermes" and Codex.
  - It audits every response receipt and keeps receipts locally ([CHANGELOG 0.2.0, 28 Sep 2026](https://github.com/Dstack-TEE/private-ai-gateway/blob/HEAD/apps/desktop/CHANGELOG.md); [cli.md](https://github.com/Dstack-TEE/private-ai-gateway/blob/HEAD/apps/desktop/docs/cli.md)).
- **OpenRouter now routes to team-operated deployments with declared, unverified metadata.** Private endpoints (28 Sep 2026), private providers and declared quantization (29 Oct 2026) — [changelog](https://openrouter.ai/docs/changelog); [private endpoints](https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/routing/private-endpoints).
- **Gateway integrity is weak.** LiteLLM 1.107.0 compromised ([#47826](https://github.com/BerriAI/litellm/issues/47826)). LiteLLM's tamper-evident callback was never released ([#38383](https://github.com/BerriAI/litellm/issues/38383)).
- **Streaming caveat.** NEAR's gateway signs streamed completions by default, and "A `gateway` signature binds the response to the gateway, not to a model report" ([NEAR](https://docs.near.ai/cloud/verification/cloud-api/response-signatures.md)). The ACI reference gives "Failed streaming requests ... no receipt" ([gaps](https://github.com/Dstack-TEE/private-ai-gateway/blob/HEAD/docs/reviews/aci-spec-conformance-gaps.md)).
- **A non-TEE quantization check exists in research.** DiFR detects 4-bit quantization at AUC > 0.999 within 300 tokens, given seed synchronisation ([arXiv 2511.20621](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.20621)).

### Inferences (directions, strongest first)
1. **Make the weights claim exact** (differentiation; high confidence).
   - Replace "signs which weights it serves" with: "For each request, the sidecar beside your model server signs the weight-file digests, quantization and engine that served it. Today no host's per-request receipt names the weights."
   - Concede that Tinfoil proves weights per deployment with hardware roots, and offer TEE-backed serving as the top tier, not the default.
2. **Sell the receipt archive, not the verifier** (both; high).
   - The sidecar fetches every host receipt within the provider's window (an hour or less at ACI reference defaults) and stores four things in the team-keyed ledger: the receipt, the cited session and attestation report, the raw request/response byte hashes (including SSE), and links to the agent session and change set.
   - Customers re-verify offline years later. Build on ACI verifiers (`@phala/aci-verifier`, `pap audit`) and teep rather than writing a new verifier.
   - Where it beats `pap`: team keys, across hosts (ACI, NEAR, self-hosted), across harnesses, tied to approvals.
3. **Emit ACI-compatible receipts from the model-server sidecar** (both; medium-high).
   - Use the `aci/1` receipt shape, and fill `model_weights_provenance` with `operator_asserted` (or `hardware_proven` under a TEE) plus the weights and runtime manifest.
   - `pap` and teep users could then verify self-hosted servers with the tools they already run. URnetwork becomes the first implementation to fill the empty claim, which fits "open all the way down".
4. **Name Phala's `pap` as the nearest product, and partner** (differentiation; high).
   - pitch4 should not claim that no one keeps receipts beside coding agents.
   - The honest line: "Phala's client keeps its own receipts on one laptop. The sidecar keeps every host's receipts, and its own, under the team's keys."
   - The same partner-first stance pitch4 takes with Block's Buzz and Goose applies to Phala.
5. **Add a "declared" tier for gateway routes** (offering; medium).
   - For OpenRouter, Kilo and OpenCode routes, record the declared provider, region, ZDR flag and quantization per call. OpenRouter now exposes these and calls them "attestation" in the declaration sense. Label them unverified.
   - Policy can still enforce on declarations, for example "only declared-ZDR EU routes for this repo".
   - This matches the three-tier record (observed, signed, attested) from 2.03 and 2.11, and avoids overclaiming proof for the most-used routes.
6. **Use the streaming and gateway-compromise facts in the pitch** (differentiation; medium).
   - Coding agents stream. Model-TEE binding is lost on NEAR's default stream path, and gateway processes can be compromised (LiteLLM 1.107.0).
   - Receipts signed at the model server and checked from the request path do not depend on the gateway or the harness.
7. **Watch list** (offering; medium). The things that would commoditise verification:
   - OpenRouter populating `required_attestation_types` or relaying receipts;
   - LiteLLM landing #29895 or asqav;
   - Coder storing responses ("In future");
   - Anthropic's 1 Jul 2027 target becoming customer-visible.
   
   If any fires, the sidecar's job shrinks to collecting and keeping those receipts, which direction 2 already covers.

### Gaps
- No evidence yet that buyers will pay for a cross-host receipt archive.
- Phala's commercial plans for `pap` (team or enterprise modes) are unknown.
- Whether ACI's maintainers would accept `operator_asserted` weights claims from non-TEE servers in verifiers' default policies is untested.
- The 2.03 auditor texts (AIUC-1 E015.4, HITRUST "version of the model used") accept self-produced, tamper-evident logs. So per-request host receipts may be a premium need rather than a requirement.
