## The trust spine (in every email)

Every email leads with the one line almost no competitor can match:

> **Encryption happens on your own machines. Agents coordinate over a post-quantum, end-to-end-encrypted network (hybrid ML-KEM-768) that carries only ciphertext. recURd — and URnetwork — never see your code, sessions, or approvals in the clear.**

Tuned per audience: agencies hear "prove it to your client without exposing their code"; security buyers hear "the relay holds no key"; eng leaders hear "nothing we host is readable by us"; open-weight teams hear "nothing leaves your control when you self-host."

## Stage-honest CTA

recURd is **design-stage** — nothing is deployed to run yet. So no email says "try it now." The CTA is **become an early design partner** (and, for the open-source segments, **read the code today**). If/when there's a runnable client or demo, point "try" at that and the CTAs can harden.

## How to use

1. Pick the segment file.
2. Choose one subject line; personalize the first line with something specific about the recipient (a client, a posted issue, their harness mix). The generic version is a fallback, not the goal.
3. Keep it short — each body is ~130–145 words on purpose. Don't add paragraphs.
4. Sign as a real person. One ask per email.

## Claims are traceable

Each file ends with an **Evidence** section mapping every nontrivial claim to a research note (`research_notes/Sidecar pitch4 differentiation directions/…`) or to `connect/` code. Don't send the Evidence section. Don't invent stats — if you want a different number, pull it from the notes first. Keep named buyers (Citi, HSBC, Barclays, Lockheed) only as they appear in note **L2-2.01**.

## What to avoid (from the research's "do not" list)

- Don't lead with audit for sub-Enterprise teams — lead with attribution (email 3).
- Don't imply "nothing leaves your control" for third-party-hosted open weights (email 4).
- Don't claim "no one keeps a record" — Happy, Agent Vault, GitLab and others do; the gap is one signed record across harnesses under customer keys.
- Don't promise a live product or a running trial.
